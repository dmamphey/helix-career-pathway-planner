User guide Feedback

Helix Career Pathway Planner provides career-development guidance and decision support. It does not determine professional eligibility, guarantee employment or replace advice from regulators, professional bodies, employers or training providers. Always confirm current mandatory requirements with the relevant official organisation.

Your data and how Helix works

Your CV stays on your device

Helix processes your document locally in your browser to build a career profile. Your CV is not uploaded to Optymum SS, and the raw document is not stored by Helix. Contact details are removed before the text is read, and the structured profile has no fields for your name, email address, telephone number or postal address.

If you choose to save progress, only the structured career profile, your saved career ids and your milestone progress are written to this browser's local storage. You can export or delete them at any time from My data.

Usage analytics

Helix can send limited, anonymous information about how the tool is used to Google Analytics. This is optional: nothing is sent unless you allow it, and you can change your answer at any time from My data.

If you allow it, what is sent is which screens you opened — using fixed names such as “recommendations” or “compare”, with the career identifiers removed from the address first — and whether a step succeeded, such as that a profile was built or a plan was exported. Those events record that something happened and carry nothing else.

Your CV, your career profile and your career plan are not sent to Google Analytics. Neither are the careers you looked at, saved, compared or planned against, your priorities, your salary expectations or your milestone notes. Helix has no field for your name, email address, telephone number or postal address, so none of those can be sent either. CV processing remains entirely local whatever you choose here.

How the matching works

Helix is rules-based career decision support. It is not artificial intelligence, and no AI service is used: the same profile always produces the same result. Careers are compared on role similarity, skill and subject overlap, education, sector exposure, experience, transferable strengths, your stated interests and professional context.

Regulated and mandatory requirements are kept separate from that comparison, so a strong alignment can never hide a registration requirement. Where Helix has not verified role-specific requirements against a current official source, it says so and links to the source instead of asserting a rule.

Helix reports three separate measures, and deliberately never merges them: background alignment (how much of a career your profile already covers), preference fit (how well it matches the priorities you stated) and transition effort (how big the move would be). None of them is a probability of getting a job.

Salary figures

Salary figures are estimates for career comparison and can vary substantially by employer, sector, location, experience, hours and working pattern. Where Helix uses a broader occupation or related-career estimate rather than a salary published for that specific job, it labels this clearly on every figure. Salary data is read from a versioned file published with this site: your browser never contacts a salary service, and the figures do not change between page views.
